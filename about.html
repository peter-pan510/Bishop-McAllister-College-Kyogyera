Our Story

From an Anglican seminary in the hills of Ankole to a thriving boarding college — four decades of faith, learning, and second chances.

Founding & History

Built on a hillside, built on faith

Bishop McAllister College Kyogyera was founded in 1983 by the Anglican Diocese of West Ankole, in the rural, hilly landscape of Kyogyera in western Uganda. The institution began its life training students on a seminary footing in the early 1990s, rooted firmly in Anglican Christian teaching.

Over the decades that followed, under the guidance of the Rev. Canon Paul Jeffries as Rector, the school grew from that seminary foundation into a highly regarded co-educational boarding college serving Senior 1 through Senior 6 — while never setting aside the seminary’s original spiritual purpose, now continued as the Bishop McAllister Anglican Seminary alongside the college.

Throughout its history, BMCK has held fast to a founding conviction: that a Christian education should be within reach of every child, including the orphaned and the vulnerable, not only those who can afford it.

Milestones

Four decades of growth

1983 — Founding

The Anglican Diocese of West Ankole establishes Bishop McAllister College in Kyogyera, western Uganda.

Early 1990s — Seminary Roots

The institution takes shape as a seminary, training students in Anglican doctrine and Christian ministry.

2006 — Global Partnership Begins

Anglican partners and sponsorship organisations abroad begin supporting BMCK students and infrastructure.

2007 — Kitchen & Computer Lab

A new kitchen and the school’s first computer laboratory open on campus.

2008 — Dining Hall & Health Facilities

A dining hall, nurse’s housing, and an examination room are added to campus.

2015 — Girls’ Dormitory Extension

Boarding capacity for girls expands to welcome more students into safe, on-campus housing.

2016 — Clean Water Project

A fresh water supply project transforms daily life for boarding students and staff.

2021 — Library Opens

A dedicated library gives students a quiet home for research and independent study.

Today — Soaring Onward

BMCK continues as a full O & A Level boarding college and Anglican seminary, still guided by its founding motto.

What Guides Us

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To provide a Christ-centred, academically rigorous secondary education that forms disciplined, compassionate leaders — regardless of a family’s ability to pay.

Our Vision

To be a leading Anglican institution in western Uganda, known for graduates who soar in faith, scholarship, and service.

Our Values

Faith, integrity, excellence, discipline, and compassion for the vulnerable — lived out daily in classroom, chapel, and dormitory alike.

GlobalPartners Supporting Our Mission

Many of our graduates — once orphaned or unable to afford school fees — have gone on to become professors, clergy, engineers, doctors, lawyers, journalists, teachers, and business leaders. Some have even returned to lecture at the universities they once attended as students. That cycle of opportunity has been made possible by partnerships with Anglican dioceses, parishes, and friends abroad who have helped fund our kitchen, computer lab, dining hall, dormitories, clean water, and library.
