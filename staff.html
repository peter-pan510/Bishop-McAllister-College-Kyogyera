Rector

Rev. Canon Paul Jeffries

The Rev. Canon Paul Jeffries serves as Rector of Bishop McAllister College Kyogyera and the Bishop McAllister Anglican Seminary, guiding the school’s spiritual and academic leadership. Under his stewardship, BMCK has grown from its seminary foundations into a highly regarded co-educational boarding college recognised across the region.
