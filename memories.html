Every day

Before the first lesson

Morning mass in our chapels

Every morning started the same way at BMCK. Before a single lesson, learners filled their chapels for morning mass. It was a quiet moment with God before the day began.

Then came assembly, then class, then prep, and the next morning the bell rang for chapel again.

Do you remember…
  • Racing to chapel before the doors closed?
  • The hymn your class always sang loudest?
  • The view from the chapel balcony on a full Sunday?
  • Sitting exams in “BMCK SEM” desks?
2011

Student leadership

Election season: choosing our prefects

Every year, BMCK learners elect their own prefects. In October 2011 the campaigns came to the compound. Candidates climbed onto a desk under the trees and made their case to the whole school.

There were speeches, slogans, cheering and a lot of nerves. The prefects chosen that day went on to lead their fellow students.

Do you remember…
  • The candidate whose speech brought the house down?
  • Your campaign slogan, or the one you chanted for a friend?
  • Waiting for the results to be announced?
2014

Sports

Champions on the hill: our football teams

BMCK football was a big deal. The men's team and the ladies' team both played for the school, and they won the inter-school competitions and carried the BMCK name to the East African level.

On match days the whole school lined the pitch, with the green hills of Ankole behind them.

2014

A Lenten tradition

The Resurrection Race

The Rector's Resurrection Race is one of BMCK's best-loved traditions. Every Lent, learners run the murram roads between the banana plantations, girls' and boys' races alike, as the school heads towards Easter.

You needed strong legs to climb those hills, and everyone came back dusty and smiling.

Do you remember…
  • That last climb back up to school?
  • Who crossed the line first in your year?
2014

Honouring excellence

Awarding the best in class

BMCK has always celebrated hard work. The best learners in each class are called to the front in front of the whole school to collect their prizes and shake hands with the guests.

For many of us, that walk to the table was one of the proudest moments of our time at school.

2016

Faith at the heart of BMCK

Walking with Christ through Lent & Holy Week

As a school founded by the Church, BMCK keeps the whole road to Easter together, from the ashes of Ash Wednesday to the candles of the Easter Vigil.

  1. Ash WednesdayAshes on the forehead as Lent begins
  2. Resurrection RaceThe Rector's race during Lent
  3. Holy ThursdayWashing of feet & Holy Communion
  4. Good FridayThe Way of the Cross
  5. Easter VigilThe night before Easter: new fire & candles
  6. Easter SundayHe is risen!

Holy Thursday: washing of the feet

Maundy Thursday

Just as Jesus washed His disciples' feet, the clergy kneel with basin and towel to wash the feet of learners. Then the school receives Holy Communion together.

Good Friday: the Way of the Cross

2016

On Good Friday the whole school walks the Stations of the Cross, a long procession in blue winding through the valleys around Kyogyera, following the cross and pausing to pray at each station.

The Easter Vigil: the night before Easter

2016

On Holy Saturday night the new fire is lit and the Paschal candle is blessed. The flame passes from candle to candle until the whole hillside glows, as the school waits together for Easter morning.

Beyond the classroom

Clubs, camps & the wider hill

Some of the best BMCK memories happened outside the classroom: at camp, over a chessboard, or at the theology side of the hill.

Scouting Club

Khaki uniforms, neckerchiefs, tents and pioneering poles lashed together by hand. The Scouts learned to camp, build and serve, and to be prepared.

Chess Club

A quieter kind of competition. The chess club trains through the term and takes on other schools in tournaments, where every move is recorded and the clock is always ticking.

Theology & the Upper Campus

BMCK began as a seminary, and that heritage lives on in its theology students and the Upper Campus, where faith, study and fellowship meet on the hill.

2019

Inter-house champions

Music, Dance & Drama: the winning house

In 2019 the inter-house Music, Dance and Drama competition was fierce, and this house took the crown. Out came the crates of sodas, and the celebration outside the classroom block went on long after the results were read.

Do you remember…
  • Your house’s song and dance that year?
  • The moment the winners were announced?
2020

Innovation & resilience

A new library, and a year like no other

In 2020 BMCK built something new: a two-storey library with blue balconies overlooking a newly paved courtyard. It gave learners a proper home for books, research and quiet study.

It was also the year of COVID-19. When learners returned, they came back in masks, keeping their distance on the same steps they used to crowd, and they kept learning.

Do you remember…
  • Your first time studying in the new library?
  • The day you finally came back to school after lockdown?
  • Masks in the BMCK blue?
2022

A day to remember

Welcoming the Bishop of West Ankole

In 2022 the Bishop of West Ankole visited BMCK. Learners lined the paved walkway in their white shirts and grey uniforms, Bibles in hand, all smiles as they waited to welcome him to the school the Diocese founded.

Many of the faces in this line are now proud OBs and OGs.

Do you remember…
  • Where you stood in the welcome line?
  • The hymn you sang as the Bishop arrived?
Home

Boarding life

The halls we called home

Every Old Boy knows which hall was his, and every Old Girl remembers Buhumuriro, the girls' wing. The boys' dormitories include Kansiime Ruhanga Hall and Bishop Bamunoba Hall, white walls and blue roofs among the trees. They were home for night prep, lights-out stories and friendships that last a lifetime.

Do you remember…
  • Your bed, your locker and your neighbours?
  • The hall captain who woke everyone for chapel?
  • Inter-hall rivalries on the pitch?
  • Climbing those long steps down to Buhumuriro?
