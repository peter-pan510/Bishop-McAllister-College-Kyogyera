S01

Old Boys & Old Girls · Saturday 26 September 2026

Kyogyera League S01: the comeback home league

Eleven teams of Old Boys and Old Girls, one for each group of classmates, came back to the hill for the first Kyogyera League. The school playground at Kitabuguma filled with team shirts, horns, blue tents and cheering from the touchline.

Every team wore its own colours. The oldest group, Bataka FC (2000–2005), came in red Coca-Cola shirts and called themselves Abataka. The youngest, Abaswaami FC, finished school only last year.

After dark, under the floodlights, Ababaagi FC (2011–2016) lifted the trophy in their orange-red shirts. Their own Nyabwina Brian scored more goals than anyone and received the Golden Boot from the head teacher and the president of the Kyogyera League.

Champions · Golden Boot

Ababaagi FC take the crown

When the floodlights came on, the league belonged to Ababaagi FC (2011–2016). In their orange-red shirts and new medals, they lifted the first Kyogyera League trophy. Their motto says it all: serving beyond the pitch.

The Golden Boot stayed with the champions too: Ababaagi’s Nyabwina Brian scored more goals than anyone. The head teacher and the president of the Kyogyera League handed it to him.

Best cheerleaders · Best smiles

Amaro FC brought the party

Nobody cheered louder than Amaro FC (2014–2019). Their Old Girls came in matching grey-blue shirts, sunglasses and bright horns, and kept the touchline singing all day.

They left with the prize for the best cheerleaders and, without any argument, the best smiles of the league.

Sponsor · Old Boy

Didia Nyakahangura

Didia Nyakahangura was one of the first students at BMCK. Today he is a well-known media personality, and he was one of the sponsors who made the league happen.

He did not only sponsor it. He put on his boots and played.

Enshera FC · 2016–2022

Enshera FC: no wins, all heart

In their school days, Enshera was one of the best teams on the hill. This time they did not win a game, but no team came back in bigger numbers.

Players, Old Girls and friends turned up in the white, black and red Enshera shirt, and they stayed to the end, still singing.

New term

Reporting day

Back to the hill: reporting day

Every new term begins with reporting day. Learners arrive with their requirements (reams of paper, brooms and more), line up under the tents, and the staff check everyone in before the term begins.

It is a busy, happy day of reunions, new faces and the start of another season on the hill.

2026

Faith · Holy Week

Easter Vigil 2026: a sea of candles

On the night before Easter, the whole school gathered on the terraced steps for the Easter Vigil. As darkness fell, the light was passed from candle to candle until every step glowed.

It is one of the most beautiful nights of the BMCK year: hymns, scripture and prayer, waiting together for the joy of Easter morning.

2023

Learning beyond the classroom

Exploring western Uganda

BMCK takes learners on study trips across the western region, and 2023 was no different. Learners climbed grassy hills in a long line of white shirts, explored rock caves in the forest and saw the landscapes of their studies up close.

Trips like these turn geography, history and biology lessons into days nobody forgets.

Music

A new home for music

Our new music room, and the Sheema music contests

BMCK has built a new music room, a bright hall with keyboards, raised steps and space for a full choir. Learners practise and learn music there.

The school now hosts the Sheema music contests, and the room also holds the school's own inter-house music competitions.

Houses

House against house

Inter-house competitions

When the houses compete, the whole school turns out. Learners in BMCK blue pack the courtyard between the new buildings to cheer, sing and dance for their house.

SU

Faith in action

Scripture Union: one of the best on campus

BMCK's Scripture Union is one of the most active fellowships at the school. Its members lead worship, study the Bible together and represent the school at SU events, where they are always among the best.
