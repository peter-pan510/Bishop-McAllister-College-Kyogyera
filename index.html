A Word From the Rector’s Office

A family rooted in faith, discipline, and hope

Welcome to Bishop McAllister College Kyogyera. For over forty years, we have offered young people from Kyogyera and beyond a home where Christian character is formed alongside academic excellence — guided today under the leadership of our Rector, the Rev. Canon Paul Jeffries.

What began as an Anglican seminary in the hills of western Uganda has grown into a thriving co-educational boarding college, without ever losing its founding purpose: to open doors of opportunity, especially for orphaned and vulnerable children, and to send out graduates who serve their communities with integrity.

Our Impact

Over 2,000 lives changed since 1983

Since its founding by the Anglican Diocese of West Ankole, BMCK has educated more than 2,000 orphaned and vulnerable Ugandan children who could not otherwise have afforded secondary school. Graduates have gone on to become professors, clergy, engineers, doctors, lawyers, journalists, teachers, and business leaders — several returning to lecture at the very universities they once attended as students.

That mission continues today through partnerships with Anglican dioceses, parishes, and friends abroad who have helped build our kitchen, computer lab, dining hall, dormitories, water supply, and library.
