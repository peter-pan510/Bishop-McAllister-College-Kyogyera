Finding us

Look for the blue sign

When you reach Kyogyera, look for the blue Bishop McAllister College signpost with the school crest. It marks the way up the hill to the college.

Visitors, parents and OBs & OGs are always welcome. Call ahead and the school office will be glad to receive you.
