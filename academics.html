Academics & Formation

A full Uganda secondary curriculum from Senior 1 to Senior 6, taught alongside daily Christian formation.

Overview

Three pathways, one formation

BMCK follows Uganda’s national secondary curriculum, delivered by a qualified and committed teaching staff, and enriched by our identity as an Anglican seminary community.

Senior 1–4

Ordinary Level (O Level)

A broad foundation across sciences, mathematics, languages, and humanities, building toward the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE).

  • Core sciences, mathematics & languages
  • Christian Religious Education throughout
  • Guided subject selection ahead of A Level
Senior 5–6

Advanced Level (A Level)

Focused subject combinations preparing students for the Uganda Certificate of Education (UACE) and university entry.

  • Sciences, arts & humanities combinations
  • University & career guidance
  • A strong record of university progression
Alongside Both Levels

Anglican Seminary Formation

For students discerning a call to ministry, spiritual formation continues through our seminary heritage.

  • Daily chapel & scripture study
  • Anglican doctrine & church history
  • Mentorship toward ministry vocations
O Level Subjects

A broad, national curriculum

Subjects offered typically include the following. Exact offerings and combinations should be confirmed with the Director of Studies’ office each academic year.

English Language Mathematics Physics Chemistry Biology Geography History Christian Religious Education Literature in English Kiswahili Agriculture Computer Studies Entrepreneurship Education
A Level Combinations

Preparing for university

Common combinations offered at Senior 5–6 typically include:

PCM — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics PCB — Physics, Chemistry, Biology HEG — History, Economics, Geography HED — History, Economics, Divinity Divinity & Religious Studies General Paper & ICT (all combinations)

Subject combinations depend on staffing and class sizes each year — please confirm current offerings with the school office.

Academic Support

The tools behind the teaching

Library

Opened in 2021, our library gives students a quiet space for research, revision, and independent reading.

Computer Laboratory

Established in 2007, our computer lab introduces students to essential digital literacy and ICT skills.

Chapel

The spiritual heart of campus, hosting daily worship and services rooted in Anglican tradition.

Dedicated Teaching Staff

A committed team of qualified teachers whose consistency has driven decades of academic results.

Beyond the Classroom

Co-curricular & boarding life

A well-rounded BMCK student grows in mind, body, and spirit — typical co-curricular activities include:

Football & Netball

Inter-house and inter-school competition throughout the year.

Music, Dance & Drama

Choir, traditional dance, and drama festivals build confidence and cultural pride.

Debate & Clubs

Debate, science, and Christian Union clubs sharpen critical thinking and leadership.

Boarding House Life

Structured dormitory life builds discipline, friendship, and shared responsibility.
