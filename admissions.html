Admissions

Everything you need to apply for Senior 1 or Senior 5 entry at Bishop McAllister College Kyogyera.

General Requirements

What you need to apply

The outline below reflects the typical requirements for Ugandan boarding secondary schools. Please confirm current cut-off points and requirements with our admissions office.

Senior 1 Entry

Coming from Primary School

  • Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) results slip
  • Original birth certificate or notification of birth
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Completed medical examination form
  • Parent/guardian interview
Senior 5 Entry

Coming from O Level

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) results slip
  • Meeting subject-combination entry points
  • Transfer/recommendation letter from previous school
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Interview with the academic office
Process

How to apply

Make Contact

Reach our admissions office by phone, email, or in person to confirm vacancies for the intake you’re applying for.

Submit Documents

Provide the required results slip, birth certificate, photographs, and medical form listed above.

Interview & Assessment

Students and parents/guardians meet with the admissions team for a short interview.

Admission Letter & Fees

Successful applicants receive an admission letter with the current fee structure and payment details.

Reporting Day

Students report to campus with boarding items (see checklist below) ready for the start of term.

Boarding Checklist

A sample list of items boarding students typically bring — confirm the current list with the matron’s office.

  • Mattress & beddings
  • School uniform & games kit
  • Trunk or box, padlock
  • Basin, toiletries & personal effects

Fee Structure

Termly fees vary by class and boarding status, and are reviewed each year. Please contact the Bursar’s office directly for the current fee structure and payment schedule.

Term Structure

Like most Ugandan schools, BMCK follows a three-term academic calendar:

  • Term I — February to May
  • Term II — May to August
  • Term III — September to November
